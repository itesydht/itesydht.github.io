Welcome, friend

It's not a cult. It's a co-op.

Tucked away on 40 peaceful acres with no cell service (on purpose), Echo Canyon is a cooperative farm where everyone shares everything: the work, the harvest, the bunkhouse, and especially your savings account.

Our spiritual guide, Brother Eddy, is a visionary, a gardener, and, according to Brother Eddy, a portal. He found enlightenment at the bottom of a pair of amber aviators and has been generously sharing it ever since. Usually with an acoustic guitar. Usually for a long time.

Membership Tiers

Probationer

Days 1 to 10
  • Bunk with a view (of other bunks)
  • Hand-stitched co-op tunic, one size fits nobody
  • Unlimited compost-turning
  • Phone placed in our "Digital Detox Shoebox"

Probationers are evaluated daily by the community. Smiling counts. So does not asking where the last group of probationers went.

Neophyte

Voted in at Day 10
  • Upgraded bunk (bottom)
  • Second tunic, for formal harvests
  • Front-row seating at Brother Eddy's nightly sing
  • Your mail, read aloud to you "for your growth"

Votes are anonymous, except Brother Eddy's, which counts as everyone's. Results are final. Appeals may be filed with Judith.

  8. Never say the word "cult." The word is "co-op." It was a vote. You weren't here yet.
