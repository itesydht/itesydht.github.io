    Welcome, friend

    It's not a cult. It's a co-op.

    Tucked away on 40 peaceful acres with no cell service (on purpose), Echo Canyon is a cooperative farm where everyone shares everything: the work, the harvest, the bunkhouse, and especially your savings account.

    Our spiritual guide, Guru Eddy, is a visionary, a gardener, and, according to Guru Eddy, a portal. He found enlightenment at the bottom of a pair of amber aviators and has been generously sharing it ever since. Usually with an acoustic guitar. Usually for a long time.

    It's a co-op. Next question.

    Can I leave whenever I want?

    Absolutely. Whenever you want to is up to you. What you want is up to Guru Eddy.

    What happens at the sister farm?

    Wonderful things. Growth. Wellness. Vitamins. Don't worry about it.

    Why is there a barn with a reinforced steel door?

    Barns are like that now. Livestock got stronger.

    Who is Judith?

    Judith is our Operations Director. She handles scheduling, logistics, your mail, your phone, your car, and your feelings about any of the above.

    Is Guru Eddy really a portal?

    He has said so many times, and he's wearing a headband, so we believe him.

    Do I have to be hot to join?

    No! All are welcome. It just, you know, helps with the vote.
